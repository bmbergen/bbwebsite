Lake Como

Como itself sets the tone on a rainy afternoon, umbrellas out along the old streets and the Cathedral's rose window catching what light there is. Around the lake, Bellagio holds its own postcard corners: the Basilica di San Giacomo's bell tower squeezed against its Romanesque facade, a narrow archway framing the water below, and a cobblestone stairway street glowing under lamplight after dark. Just outside town, Villa Melzi d'Eril's gardens run along the shore beneath a promenade of pollarded plane trees, a cherub fountain rising from a bed of water lilies, and a stone path winding past maples and rhododendrons. Across the water, Villa Carlotta answers with its own set pieces — a weathered triton fountain, a rose-draped staircase, terraced flower beds, a hilltop villa glimpsed through the greenery, and citrus ripening on the branch. The trip ends in Varenna, where a footbridge named for a former mayor leads onto the town's famous red-railed walkway, hugging the cliff just above the water.
